Choose all five students from a group of five. There is exactly one possible team: everybody. Our combination formula should agree. Put five into both places, and the denominator contains five factorial times zero factorial. After the five factorials cancel, the answer is one divided by zero factorial. For this formula to include choosing everybody, we define zero factorial as one. And there is a picture behind that definition. An empty box has one arrangement: the empty arrangement. No objects does not mean no possible outcome. It means the one outcome contains nothing. This also keeps the factorial pattern consistent at its boundary. Now we can bring the whole story together.
