We started with a simple question. Why is carbon dioxide straight, while water is bent? The answer wasn't the number of atoms. It was how the electrons were arranged. VSEPR gave us a way to predict the shape, just by counting regions around the central atom. Hybridization gave us a picture of orbitals pointing along those bonds. And once we knew the shape, we could see why some molecules are polar and others are not. A Lewis structure may look flat on paper. But molecules live in three dimensions. And that third dimension changes everything: how molecules fit together, how they attract each other, and how they react.
