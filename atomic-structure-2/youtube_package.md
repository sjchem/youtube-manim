## Title

Why Electrons Don’t Orbit the Nucleus

## Description

If electrons do not follow planetary orbits, what replaces that picture?

We start where most of us did: Bohr's atom, with its fixed radius, fixed energy, fixed speed and the 2n² rule. Then we spend every one of those numbers. Bohr's own electron speed becomes a de Broglie wavelength — which turns out to be exactly the circumference of his first orbit, so the quantisation he had to assume simply falls out of a wave closing on itself. His radius and speed become the pair Heisenberg forbids. And his 2, 8, 18 comes back at the end, derived from orbitals rather than memorised.

Along the way: the double slit run one electron at a time, the wave function and what squaring it means, the shapes of s, p and d orbitals computed from the real hydrogen solutions, all four quantum numbers built as an address, Pauli and Hund discovered through the atoms that force them, and Madelung's rule explaining why potassium starts a fourth shell before the third one is full.

Build hydrogen, helium, carbon, oxygen, neon, sodium and potassium by seeing why each next electron occupies the state it does. The next video explores the structure of the periodic table.

This is Part 2 of our atomic structure series. Part 1 follows the experiments that revealed the nucleus and allowed energies.

Keep following physics, chemistry and mathematics.
