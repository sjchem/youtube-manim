# Deferred chapter — The lowest total energy — 25:10–25:55

One final check keeps the filling diagram honest. For chromium, a simple ordering guide suggests four electrons in three d and two in four s. The observed dominant ground-state configuration is instead three d to the fifth, four s to the first.

Copper gives another warning: three d to the tenth, four s to the first. Nearby arrangements compete in total energy.

Half-filled and filled subshells are useful landmarks, but not a universal shortcut that replaces the calculation. The lowest total energy wins. The diagonal arrows were always a guide, never the underlying law.
