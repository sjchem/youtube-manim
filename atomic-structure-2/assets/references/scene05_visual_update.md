# Scene 5 visual update

The apparatus is confined to approximately y = ±2.7. Main headings and
equations are centred at y = 3.55; lower captions at y = -3.60. Initial
component labels are removed before the explanatory captions appear.
The wave map occupies y = ±2.45 and ends before the detector panel.

Visual colour contract:
- Gold: the explicitly classical-particle comparison.
- Cyan / violet: contributions from the upper / lower opening.
- Mint with white centres: recorded electron detections.
- Coral: records from a fresh run with a path detector.

Source electrons move only inside the source, so reading holds remain active
without suggesting extra emissions between the individual-electron shots.
Six localized detections each follow a separate propagating amplitude pulse.
No electron is shown following a classical trajectory through either slit.
Classical pellets alone have visible trajectories.

The screen probability remains the existing Gaussian two-slit model in
utils/quantum_examples.py. The cloud narrows the two Gaussian envelopes near
their openings and interpolates toward that far-field model; it is a
schematic visual, not a calibrated propagation solver. Moving bright crests
show phase over a fixed intensity envelope. At the screen the underlying
amplitudes are sqrt(g/2) exp(±iky), matching the existing probability formula.
Soft cropping keeps the field out of the caption area.

With a recorded path, the cloud and new screen records use the incoherent
sum of intensities. Previous records fade before the new run accumulates.
They are never moved to turn the old fringes into a different distribution.

The closing ring oscillates radially with fixed nodes, maintaining its
standing-wave analogy. Updaters are cleared before the chapter handoff.
Narration and the 90-second scene window are unchanged.
