[warm, conversational delivery] Here is a picture of a gas that is wrong in a quiet way. It imagines every molecule at one temperature moving at the same speed. Watch two identical molecules, approaching at exactly the same speed. [short pause] They collide. The collision is elastic: the total kinetic energy is conserved, and so is the total momentum. [short pause] [curious] But look at the individual speeds. One molecule leaves faster. The other leaves slower. Energy has passed from one to the other. In air, each molecule suffers billions of collisions every second. So its speed keeps changing, again and again. [pause] No molecule keeps one speed for long. The real question is: what does the whole crowd look like?
