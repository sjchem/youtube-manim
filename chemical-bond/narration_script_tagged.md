## Scene 13 — Metallic bonding — 19:30–21:00

[warm, conversational delivery] Copper presents a different problem: many closely packed atoms, with electrons in quantum states extending through the solid. It is a simplified picture of electrons spread through the entire solid. The sea attracts every core, and every core attracts the sea. That shared attraction is the metallic bond. Apply a voltage. Electrons acquire a small drift toward the positive side; conventional current points the other way. [curious] Now hit the metal. A layer of cores slides over the next, but the sea simply flows along. The metal bends instead of breaking. Try the same with salt. [warmly] Shared, transferred, or delocalised: three different ways for electrons to lower the energy of the whole.

## Scene 14 — Bond length and strength — 21:00–23:30

[warm, conversational delivery] Let's return to the energy valley. Every bond has one, and two numbers describe it.
