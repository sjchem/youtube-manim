In the last video, the periodic table showed us that some atoms hold their electrons tightly, while others let them go easily. Sodium gives up its outer electron for a modest price. Chlorine releases energy when it gains one. So what happens when atoms like these actually meet? You may have learned three kinds of bond, covalent, ionic and metallic, each with its own list of rules to memorise. Instead, we will build all three from scratch, using only electrons, nuclei and energy. And one idea will keep coming back. A bond forms when the whole system ends up with lower energy than the atoms had apart.
