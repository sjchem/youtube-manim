Last time, we built this table from one question: where can the next electron go? Filling s, p, d and f subshells gave us four blocks, and the blocks gave the table its shape. Now we ask a new question. Not why the table looks like this, but what it lets us predict. Let the symbols fade. Keep only the outlines. Behind every tile is an atom. And atoms differ in size, in how tightly they hold electrons, and in how strongly they attract more. Colour each tile by one of those properties, and the table lights up in smooth patterns. Those patterns are called periodic trends. You could memorise them as arrows. But we will do something better. We will discover a few physical ideas, and use them again and again. How far are the outer electrons from the nucleus? How strongly does the nucleus pull on them? And how are the electrons arranged? Three questions. Almost every trend in this video comes from them.
