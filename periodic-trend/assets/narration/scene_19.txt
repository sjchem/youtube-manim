Let the table fade. Keep just two atoms: sodium and chlorine. The periodic table doesn't just tell us what the elements are. It helps us predict how they behave. From the size of atoms to the energy needed to remove an electron, from electronegativity to reactivity, the patterns are connected. And when two atoms come close enough to interact, these same ideas help decide how they bond. Which brings us to the next question: why do atoms bond at all?
