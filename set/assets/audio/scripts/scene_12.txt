Choose a universe U and imagine testing one object, x, inside it. Every rule in this video asks where that object belongs. A is a subset of B when every x in A also belongs to B. So the subset symbol makes a promise about all of A's members. For union, x passes when it belongs to A or B. That or includes the overlap. For intersection, x passes only when it belongs to A and B. The picture of two circles was showing these membership tests all along. For a complement, we must keep the universe in view. The object x belongs to the complement of A when it is in U and is outside A. Now read De Morgan's two laws together. Negating a union gives the intersection of the complements. Negating an intersection gives the union of the complements. In each case, the equation states that the two sides accept exactly the same members.
