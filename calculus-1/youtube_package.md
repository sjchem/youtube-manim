## Description

Calculus begins with a strange problem: how do you measure change at one exact instant, when every ordinary measurement needs two moments to compare? This film builds the answer visually — from a car easing away from a red light, through the idea of a limit, a removable hole in a graph, the three faces of continuity, a geometric proof of `lim(x→0) sin(x)/x = 1`, and a hand-built derivative of `x²` — to the deepest idea in the video: zoom into any differentiable curve far enough, and it becomes indistinguishable from its tangent line. That tangent is the local model; its slope is the derivative.

Part 1 of a series. Part 2 is **From Derivatives to Integrals**.
