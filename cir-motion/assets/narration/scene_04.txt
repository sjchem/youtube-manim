Picture an object moving around a circle of radius r. At every instant, it has a definite velocity. But which way does that velocity point? Draw the position vector, r, from the centre of the circle to the object. The velocity points along the tangent: the line that just touches the circle at that point. And the tangent is always perpendicular to the radius. Velocity is perpendicular to r. So the object is never moving toward the centre, and never directly outward. At each moment, it's heading off along the tangent. As it moves on, the tangent turns, and the velocity turns with it. Same length, new direction, every instant.
