### Scene 08 · The momentum of a system · 09:19 (89.8s)

*P = M v_cm; carts 2 kg × 3, 3 kg × −1 → 0.6 m/s through a collision* — `manim_scenes.scene_08_momentum.Scene08Momentum`

The momentum of a system. Two carts sit on a 3D frictionless track with gold velocity arrows. The definitions build above them: P is the sum of m_i v_i; differentiating M R_cm = sum m_i r_i gives P = M v_cm; dP/dt = F_ext; with no external force, P is constant. ## Chapter — Rotational dynamics, work and energy

### Scene 21 · Newton's second law for rotation · 26:46 (86.5s)

*F_t = m r α → τ = m r² α → Σ: τ_net = I α* — `manim_scenes.scene_21_tau_i_alpha.Scene21TauIAlpha`

Newton's second law for rotation. A disc faces the viewer on a 3D stage with one gold particle m at distance r. A green force on it splits into a dim radial part and an outlined white tangential part F_t. The radius r and its arrow are outlined magenta. The derivation builds line by line on the right: F_t = m a_t, a_t = r alpha, so F_t = m r alpha; multiplied by r, the torque on that particle is m r^2 alpha. Every particle then lights up with its own tangential push; alpha comes out of the sum, the sum of m r^2 is I, the internal torques cancel: tau_net = I alpha, boxed. A three-row dictionary pairs torque with force, I with mass, alpha with a; short comparisons explain alpha = tau / I.

### Scene 22 · Spinning up a disc · 28:13 (58.6s)

*4 kg, 0.5 m disc on an axle (3D): I = 0.5, τ = 1 N m → α = 2* — `manim_scenes.scene_22_disc.Scene22Disc`

Spinning up a disc. A uniform solid disc (4 kg, 0.5 m) sits on a frictionless axle on a 3D stand. I = 1/2 M R^2 = 0.5 kg m^2 is worked out. ### Scene 23 · Rotational energy and work · 29:11 (107.5s)

*Σ ½ m v² → ½ I ω²; 16 J = W = τ Δθ; P = τ ω* — `manim_scenes.scene_23_energy.Scene23Energy`

Rotational energy and work. A disc spins in place on a 3D stand: its centre stays put, yet it carries energy. Gold particles on its face light up with velocity arrows growing with r. The derivation builds: K_i = 1/2 m_i v_i^2, v_i = r_i omega, sum, omega^2 out, the sum is I: K_rot = 1/2 I omega^2 (boxed, beside 1/2 M v^2). The driven disc: K = 1/2 x 0.5 x 8^2 = 16 J, a coral bar. Work by a torque through an angle, W = tau delta theta = 1 x 16 = 16 J, fills a matching bar. Then W as the integral of tau d theta and the power P = tau omega.

## Chapter — Angular momentum and its conservation

### Scene 24 · Angular momentum · 30:59 (106.5s)

*L = r × p (3D); straight line, circle, rigid body; τ = dL/dt* — `manim_scenes.scene_24_angular_momentum.Scene24AngularMomentum`

Angular momentum. On a 3D floor a particle glides in a straight line past an origin O: teal r, gold p and the rose L = r x p standing up from O. The dashed lever arm d_perp is the same at every instant, so the live |L| readout never changes. The particle then circles O: L = m r v = m r^2 omega. A disc spins with omega and L = I omega together on its axis. A tilted dumbbell turning about a vertical axle shows the general case: its L (computed as the sum of m r x v) sweeps round the axle while omega stays put; only the component along the axle equals I omega. tau = dL/dt and tau = I alpha close the scene.

### Scene 25 · The spinning skater · 32:45 (97.8s)

*3D skater: I 4 → 2, ω 2 → 4, L = 8; K 8 → 16 J* — `manim_scenes.scene_25_skater.Scene25Skater`

### Scene 27 · The energy of rolling · 35:38 (60.2s)

### Scene 28 · The race down the ramp · 36:38 (93.7s)
