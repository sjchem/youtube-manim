Acceleration a becomes angular acceleration alpha, the rate of change of omega. [short pause] And time is still just time. [short pause] When angular acceleration is constant, angular velocity changes by alpha t. Add that change to the initial spin: omega equals omega zero plus alpha t. The angle reached is theta zero, plus omega zero t, plus one half alpha t squared. The initial spin and the acceleration each contribute to the angle turned. The third equation links speed and angle without time: omega squared equals omega zero squared, plus two alpha times theta minus theta zero. [pause] [warmly] Same structure, new symbols.
