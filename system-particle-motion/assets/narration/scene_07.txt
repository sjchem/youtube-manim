Here's a dramatic test. A firework shell is launched. Ignoring air resistance, it follows a parabola. At the top, it bursts. Fragments fly in every direction. The burst forces are internal. They push the fragments apart, but in equal and opposite pairs. The only external force is still gravity, the same total pull as before. So the centre of mass of all the fragments carries on along the original parabola, as if nothing had happened. That holds until a fragment hits the ground. Then the ground pushes on it, a new external force, and the centre of mass leaves the parabola. Internal forces can rearrange a system. Only external forces can change the motion of its centre of mass.
