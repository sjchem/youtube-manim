## Scientific checks

The numerical models remain the source of the worked values: 3 m COM, 0.6 m/s cart COM speed, 8 rad/s and 16 rad spin-up, 0.5 kg m² disc inertia, 1 N m torque, 2 rad/s² angular acceleration, 16 J rotational energy, the skater's 8 J internal work, and the ramp accelerations. The visual contract checks include the actual couple force cross products and a finite-difference check of the scene's actual rim-point trajectory at contact.

Assumptions are spoken where needed: negligible air resistance for projectile COM paths, a frictionless cart track and disc axle, negligible beam mass, fixed-mass systems, specified origins and axes, fixed inertia for τ = Iα, and rolling without slipping on a stationary surface. Static friction supplies rolling torque without dissipative contact work in the ideal rigid-body model.
