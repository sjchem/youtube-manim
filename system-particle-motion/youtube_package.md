## Description

Push a box, and it slides. Push a door, and it turns. Push a cylinder, and it
does both. Why?

Episode 03 of *Visualized Physics* builds rotational mechanics from the ground
up, with 3D animation and every number checked: the centre of mass (and why a
tumbling hammer's centre still flies a parabola), internal forces that cancel,
an exploding firework, the cross product, torque and the lever arm, couples
and equilibrium, moment of inertia (ring vs disc, the parallel and
perpendicular axis theorems), rotational kinematics, τ = Iα, rotational
energy and work, angular momentum and the spinning skater, and rolling — ending
in a ramp race between a cylinder, a ring and a sliding block.

Covers NCERT Class 11 Physics, "System of Particles and Rotational Motion".
