[warm, conversational delivery] We began with one glass, and a simple question: how concentrated is it? [short pause] [warmly] Now every number has a meaning you can see. Molarity: moles of solute per litre of solution. Molality: moles per kilogram of solvent. Mole fraction: a share of the whole population. Mass percent: a share of the whole mass. Normality: reaction equivalents per litre. And ppm: tiny ratios, on a stated basis. Each one tells us something different about the same chemical system. [pause] [thoughtful] So next time you meet a concentration formula, don't start by memorising it. Ask one question. [short pause] [softly] What are we actually comparing? [pause] [warmly] Once you can see that, the mathematics follows naturally.
