We've met each measure on its own. Now let's connect them. Molarity lives in litres of solution. Molality lives in kilograms of solvent. To connect them, we need a bridge from volume to mass. That bridge is density. Our solution: one hundred and ninety-eight grams, in one hundred and ninety-one millilitres. One point zero three seven grams per millilitre.
