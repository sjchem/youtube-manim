## Description

This connected visual course builds trigonometry from motion instead of memorization. One rotating point becomes a coordinate, a right triangle, a unit-circle point, a sine wave, a sound signal, a Fourier component, and finally Euler’s complex exponential.

The course covers coordinates and quadrants, degrees and radians, Pythagoras, SOH–CAH–TOA, all six trigonometric functions, exact special angles, unit-circle signs, sine and cosine graphs, transformations, inverse trig functions, the major identity families, laws of sines and cosines, sound, Fourier analysis, and Euler’s formula.
