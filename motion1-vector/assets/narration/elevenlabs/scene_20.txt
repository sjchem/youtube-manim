[warm, conversational delivery] First, the horizontal motion. Without air resistance, nothing pushes or pulls the ball sideways. So a x is zero, and the horizontal velocity never changes. v x equals v zero cosine theta, all the way through the flight. Then the horizontal position grows steadily: x equals v zero cosine theta times t. Watch the shadow on the ground. Snapshots at equal time intervals are equally spaced. For our throw, x equals fourteen point one four t. After one second: fourteen point one four metres. After two seconds: twenty-eight point two eight metres. Equal distances in equal times. Gravity pulls down the whole time, but it pulls straight down. It has no sideways part to slow the ball. So the horizontal motion is the easy part. Uniform, and predictable. [pause] [warmly] The vertical motion follows a very different pattern.
