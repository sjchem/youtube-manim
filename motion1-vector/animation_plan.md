29 scenes, 41:42, grouped into the brief's 9 chapters. **3D** marks scenes
drawn on the projected `Stage` (real perspective, depth sorting, slow camera
sway). Colour key: horizontal blue · vertical rose · velocity gold ·
acceleration orange · position teal · resultant green · second vector / time
purple · trap red.

## Revised visual direction

Scene headings hold for 3 seconds and fade over 0.7 seconds while the demonstration continues. Chapter cards also disappear before their main demonstration. Keep narration prose off screen: labels, quantities, essential equations and worked values carry the picture. Shared sphere lighting connects side views and projected 3D shots. Comparative flights share elapsed physical time; shorter flights land first and remain on the ground.

Scene 11 now uses a perspective stage with a ruled floor, polished light markers, parallel projection rays, blue and rose component arrows, and a camera move as the vertical component slides tip to tail. The diagonal arrow remains one quantity. Its equations appear beside the geometry. The sled narration distinguishes weight from normal contact force.

The tables below describe the teaching beats; quoted explanatory phrases in older planning notes are narration prompts, not captions to restore.
