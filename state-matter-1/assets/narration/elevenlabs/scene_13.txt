[warm, conversational delivery] But molecules don't only move along x. They fly in every direction. [short pause] Gather every velocity arrow at one point. Their tips form a fuzzy ball. No direction is special. Each velocity has three parts: v x, v y and v z. By Pythagoras in three dimensions, v squared equals v x squared plus v y squared plus v z squared. Average over the molecules. Small samples fluctuate, but at equilibrium, with no preferred direction, the three mean squares are equal. So the average of v x squared is exactly one third of the average of v squared. [short pause] [warmly] That one third is three dimensional space, showing up in the equation. Put it into our pressure. P V equals one third N m times the average of v squared. This is the pressure equation of the kinetic theory of gases.
