[short pause] [emphasized] More momentum per hit, and more hits per second. The pressure climbs. Pressure is proportional to absolute temperature. P one over T one equals P two over T two. Heat a sealed container from three hundred to three hundred and sixty kelvin, and a pressure of one bar becomes one point two bar. Both effects grow with speed, so the pressure grows with speed squared, which is proportional to temperature. [pause] A sealed, rigid gas cylinder shows this directly. With a fixed amount of gas, warming raises its pressure because molecules transfer momentum to the walls faster.
